Privacy

Buzzed Cut · Updated 3 November 2026

Buzzed Cut is an AI entertainment demo. This notice explains how we use the information you provide. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@buzzedcut.com.

What we use

We process the video and audio you upload, the name, age and optional caption you enter, your questions, the generated replies, scores and exported show. Age helps us adapt the tone. We also use basic connection information, such as IP addresses, for rate limits and troubleshooting.

Why we use it

We use this information to analyse the uploaded clip, create your show, answer questions, keep the result consistent when the same file is submitted again, and provide replay and downloads. Processing needed to provide the show you request is part of delivering that service; proportionate security and troubleshooting support the operation of the demo. We do not sell your uploaded videos or use them for advertising.

Who processes it

Meta processes clips and model requests. Modal hosts the application, stores show data and serves model requests. ElevenLabs receives the text needed to generate judge voices. These providers process relevant data under their own service terms and privacy arrangements, and processing may take place outside the EU/EEA. Contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your request.

Access to your show

Your upload is not added to a public gallery. Your show and download have unlisted links. Anyone who has one of those links can access it; this prototype does not require account sign-in. Please treat a show link like a shareable link, rather than a private vault.

Saved shows and downloads

The saved-show list and download references are kept in this browser’s local storage. We show the newest 50 entries. Clearing browser data removes those local references, not the server files. Server copies remain available for replay and download while this demo operates, unless removed at your request or as part of closing the demo. Email us with the show link to request removal.

Children

A parent or guardian must submit videos for contestants under 16 and confirm this in the upload form. Do not submit personal information about a child without the necessary authority. The age-aware show avoids adult language for younger contestants.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable, data-protection law gives you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and a portable copy of your data. Where processing depends on consent, you can withdraw it. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email info@buzzedcut.com with your show link and what you need. We may need information to confirm you are entitled to make the request.

AI scores

Judges and scores are generated entertainment opinions. They are not professional assessments or decisions about employment, education or another entitlement. We do not identify people through face recognition. A resubmission of the same uploaded file may get new dialogue while keeping its original scores.

Storage and safeguards

The website uses HTTPS. Provider keys remain on the server. Uploaded files and questions are not placed in the public saved-show list. No system can guarantee absolute security or confidentiality, and provider-held data is subject to the provider’s own retention arrangements.
